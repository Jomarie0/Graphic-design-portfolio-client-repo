05 / PROFESSIONAL INTRODUCTION

Design beyond
appearance.

I am a junior graphic designer with an interest in visual communication, digital creativity, and design production.

My work focuses on creating visually engaging designs that balance creativity, functionality, and attention to detail. I have experience exploring different areas of graphic design, particularly sportswear and sublimation jersey layouts, promotional materials, and digital advertising creatives. I enjoy transforming ideas and references into organized, purposeful visual compositions.

With a growing foundation in Adobe Photoshop, Canva, and Figma, I continuously work on improving my technical abilities, exploring creative approaches, and expanding my understanding of design. I believe that effective design goes beyond appearance: it communicates ideas, serves a purpose, and creates a meaningful visual experience.
